# GO65 - Creative Peripheral Workflow

## Goal

GO65 pushes the creative journey one level deeper: not only identifying the obvious core tools, but also checking the peripheral workflow that actually makes the craft efficient.

## What changes

- The creative discovery step now asks specialty-specific questions for:
  - UI/product handoff
  - content distribution and measurement
  - creative ops and studio follow-through
  - reusable brand-system assets
  - illustration/3D pipeline continuity
- Those questions are tied to real peripheral tool families already present in the catalog.
- The final insights now convert those answers into concrete focus areas instead of leaving them as isolated questionnaire signals.

## Why it matters

The strongest creative stack is often not defined by the main app alone. It is defined by the hidden chain around it: plugins, templates, presets, review, rights, publishing, and operational follow-through.
